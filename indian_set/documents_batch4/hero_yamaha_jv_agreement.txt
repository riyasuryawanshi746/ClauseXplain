Article 2. COMMON UNDESTANDING The Parties agree on the following as common understanding for the establishment of the Company:

S}

The basic philosophy ofthe Parties is to establish a Company manufacturing Hub motors for E-bikes that supplies outstanding products, understand and respect the philosophy of both Parties regarding manufacturing, sincerely respond to customers' demands for product performance, quality, and prices, and realize competitive and sustainable manufacturing.

The philosophy in manufacturing is the wisdom and culture of manufacturing technology that both Parties have cultivated over many years, and it has been nurtured by the environment and history surrounding the Parties. Therefore, the Parties shall understand that there are differences between the Parties, and respect the other's thoughts. On that basis, each Party shall make decisions in order to realize the basic philosophy.

improving the products offered by the Company so as to give added value to the

Company shall enhance their manufacturing capabilities.
